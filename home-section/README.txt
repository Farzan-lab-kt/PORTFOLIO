Minecraft-inspired portfolio Home section.

Assets supplied by the user are used for the background video, portal video,
portal sound, home music, Minecraft font, title artwork, button artwork, and
hover sound.

Portal transition behavior:
- Clicking a navigation button starts the supplied portal video and portal sound together.
- The supplied portal video loops until the portal sound ends.
- Navigation is locked during the transition.
- The portal layer waits for the portal video's first frame before becoming visible,
  preventing a black-screen flash.
- The hover sound plays only when the pointer enters a navigation button.

The Home section remains independent from the other portfolio sections.
